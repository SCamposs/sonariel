# Sonariel

## Register

product

## Users

Audio creators using a connected AI agent to direct local music and multilingual voice engines on Windows. They need to follow CPU tasks, compare candidates, preserve decisions, process WAVs and export results, with an optional Audacity handoff. Codex is the client exercised in this project; other MCP clients require their own validation.

## Product Purpose

Connect local music generation, voice synthesis, native DSP and project/version management through one reproducible workflow shared by the desktop and a stdio MCP server. Creative direction remains in the connected agent. Audio engines execute locally; the agent's language model is separate and may use a remote service. Sonariel does not provide its own autonomous agent.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, precise, atmospheric. Tactical field equipment translated into practical desktop software.

## Anti-references

Marketing landing pages, generic SaaS metric grids, neon cyberpunk, fantasy HUDs, characters, copied game branding, decorative fake telemetry.

## Design Principles

- Every state is observable and backed by the local core.
- Installation and recovery are understandable, repeatable operations.
- Listening and comparison stay close to version history.
- Contrast, typography and framing carry the atmosphere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, keyboard controls, semantic forms, status conveyed with text as well as color, reduced motion, no essential animation.
