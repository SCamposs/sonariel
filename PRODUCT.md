# Sonariel

## Register

product

## Users

Music creators using Codex to direct a local music engine on Windows. They need to follow long CPU tasks, compare candidates, preserve decisions and hand results to Audacity.

## Product Purpose

Manage a reproducible local engine and expose one persistent workflow to a desktop workspace and a stdio MCP server. Creative direction remains in Codex.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, precise, atmospheric. Tactical field equipment translated into practical desktop software.

## Anti-references

Marketing landing pages, generic SaaS metric grids, neon cyberpunk, fantasy HUDs, characters, copied game branding, decorative fake telemetry.

## Design Principles

- Every state is observable and backed by the local core.
- Installation and recovery are understandable, repeatable operations.
- Listening and comparison stay close to version history.
- Contrast, typography and framing carry the atmosphere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, keyboard controls, semantic forms, status conveyed with text as well as color, reduced motion, no essential animation.
